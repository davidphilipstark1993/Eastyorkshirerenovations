Beverley property types

Renovating Beverley's period townhouses and modern homes.

Beverley's centre is built around handsome Georgian and Victorian townhouses close to the Minster and Saturday Market, many within conservation areas where sympathetic materials and finishes matter as much as the end result. Around the edges of town, Molescroft and the newer estates off the Beverley bypass offer more straightforward modern semis and detached family homes.

Period townhouses in and around Beverley often need careful re-plastering to match original wall profiles, joinery that respects existing skirting and architrave detailing, and bathroom or kitchen renovations that work within smaller, more irregular room shapes than you'd find in a modern build. Newer homes in Molescroft tend to need more straightforward kitchen and bathroom upgrades and full refurbishments as they age.

We also cover the villages just outside Beverley - Cherry Burton, Molescroft's outer edges and the surrounding countryside - for bathroom, kitchen and full renovation work.
