Quick answers

About East Yorkshire Renovation.

Who is East Yorkshire Renovation?

A renovation and damp proofing firm based in Hessle, trading since 2025. We carry out damp surveys and treatment, kitchen and bathroom renovations, full house renovations, and the plastering, joinery, electrical work and decorating that go with them. We also take on garage conversions, garden rooms, orangeries, conservatory transformations and outdoor kitchens.

Where are you based, and which areas do you cover?

Our only base is on Station Road in Hessle (HU13 0BG); we have no other offices. We work on homes within about 30 miles of Hessle: Hull, Beverley, Cottingham and the villages of the East Riding, Goole and Howden, and across the Humber Bridge in North Lincolnshire (Barton-upon-Humber, Brigg, Scunthorpe) and Grimsby. If you’re near the edge, send us your postcode.

What problems do you help with?

Damp walls, condensation and black mould; rising and penetrating damp; tired or failing kitchens and bathrooms; houses that need a full refurbishment after a purchase or between tenants; and homes that need more space, through a garage conversion, garden room or orangery. For damp we find the cause first, then quote for the fix.

How do I get a quote?

Call 07498 951487, send photos on WhatsApp, or use the quote form. We reply within 3 hours during opening hours (Monday to Friday, 8am to 4:30pm), visit to look at the job, and give you a written quote before any work starts. For damp, book a £119 damp survey; the fee is deducted from the treatment if you go ahead.

What does a bathroom renovation cost?

A typical full bathroom renovation we carry out comes to around £6,000. The room size, suite, tiles and what we find behind the old tiles all change that, so we confirm the price in a written quote. See kitchen and bathroom renovation costs.

What makes you different from other renovation firms?

One firm handles the whole job, including the plastering, making good and decorating, so you have one point of contact. The owner carries out the damp surveys and writes the reports, so the person who diagnoses the damp also quotes for the work and answers for it afterwards.
