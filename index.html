Damp and property problems

Damp surveys and treatment, cause first.

Damp patches, black mould, peeling paint or “damp noted” on a mortgage survey: we find out what is actually causing it before recommending anything. A damp survey costs £119, deducted from the treatment if you go ahead with our quote.

Because we are a renovation firm, we also do the replastering, making good and decorating ourselves, so the room is finished, not just treated. Damp proofing work is backed by written guarantees of up to 30 years.
