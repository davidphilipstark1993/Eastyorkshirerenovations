Garden room guides

Garden office design ideas.

A few design choices make the biggest difference to how well a garden office actually works day to day.

Request a Free Quote More about garden room guides

Layout and light

Position your desk to get good natural light without screen glare — usually side-on to a large window rather than directly facing it. Bi-fold or large sliding doors work well if you want the option to open the space up in good weather.

Storage and connectivity

Built-in shelving and a dedicated cable management plan keep a small office tidy. Running network cabling back to your house's router, rather than relying on Wi-Fi through a garden structure, gives more reliable connectivity for video calls.
