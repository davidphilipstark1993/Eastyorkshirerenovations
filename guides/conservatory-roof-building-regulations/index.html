Conservatory guides

Do conservatory roof replacements need building regulations approval?

This comes down to the conservatory exemption — whether your finished room still qualifies for it, or whether the transformation removes that status.

Request a Free Quote More about conservatory guides

The conservatory exemption

Conservatories can be exempt from building regulations if they meet specific criteria, broadly: they're independently heated with separate controls, thermally separated from the main house by external-quality doors, and under a certain floor area.

When a roof replacement changes that

A straightforward roof-only replacement that keeps the room's heating and thermal separation as it was often doesn't affect the exemption. A fuller transformation that removes that separation — for example, opening the room fully into the house or putting it on the main heating system — can mean building regulations now apply to the new roof, glazing and electrics.

Frequently asked questions

FAQs.

How do I know if my conservatory currently qualifies for the exemption?

We assess this as part of the survey, based on how it's currently heated and separated from the rest of the house.

Will a full rebuild always need building regulations approval?

In most cases, yes, since a full rebuild typically changes the heating and thermal separation arrangement.
