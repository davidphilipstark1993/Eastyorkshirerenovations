How much does a kitchen renovation cost in Hull?

A guide to what affects kitchen renovation pricing in Hull and East Yorkshire, and the questions worth asking before you budget.

Online cost calculators and national "average kitchen cost" articles rarely reflect what you'll actually pay locally, because so much depends on the size of your kitchen, the scope of work and the finish you choose. This guide walks through the main factors so you know what questions to ask when you get quotes.

What affects the cost of a kitchen renovation

  • Size and layout: a small galley kitchen costs less to fit than a large open-plan kitchen-diner, and moving the layout around (relocating a sink or hob) adds plumbing and electrical work.
  • Structural work: knocking through to a dining room or hallway adds building work and potentially a structural beam, on top of the kitchen fit itself.
  • Cabinet material: MDF and laminate cabinets cost less than solid wood or painted shaker-style units.
  • Worktop material: laminate is the most affordable option; quartz, granite and marble-effect worktops cost more but are more durable.
  • Appliances: integrated, built-in appliances cost more than freestanding ones, and the number of appliances (oven, hob, extractor, dishwasher, fridge-freezer) adds up.
  • Tiling extent: a splashback behind the hob costs less than full wall tiling.
  • Condition of existing plumbing and electrics: older properties sometimes need first-fix work redone before a new kitchen can go in.

Hidden costs to budget for

  • Re-plastering walls and ceilings once old units and tiling are removed.
  • Consumer unit or electrical upgrades if the existing wiring doesn't meet current regulations.
  • Flooring repairs or replacement once old units are stripped out.
  • Skip hire and waste disposal.
  • Temporary kitchen facilities if you're staying in the property during the work.

Getting an accurate quote

The most reliable way to budget for a kitchen renovation is a site visit, where we can measure the space, talk through layout options and give you an itemised quote rather than a rough estimate.

How much does a bathroom renovation cost in East Yorkshire?

What drives bathroom renovation pricing in Hull and East Yorkshire, and the hidden costs worth planning for.

Bathroom renovation costs vary more than most people expect, mainly because of what's happening behind the tiles rather than what you can see. This guide covers the main cost drivers so you can budget realistically before getting quotes.

What affects the cost of a bathroom renovation

  • Room size: a small ensuite costs less to renovate than a full family bathroom.
  • Suite quality: budget bath, basin and toilet suites cost less than premium fixtures and designer fittings.
  • Wet room vs standard shower: a level-access wet room conversion involves more structural and waterproofing work than fitting a shower over a bath.
  • Tiling extent: full-height tiling on every wall costs more than half-height tiling with painted walls above.
  • Waterproofing and tanking: proper tanking behind tiled shower areas is essential and adds to the cost, but it's not somewhere to cut corners.
  • Moving plumbing: relocating the toilet, bath or basin adds plumbing work compared with replacing fittings in the same positions.
  • Ventilation: extractor fan installation or upgrades, particularly in bathrooms without an external window.

Hidden costs to budget for

  • Rotten or damaged subfloor discovered once old flooring is removed.
  • Old plumbing needing full replacement rather than reuse.
  • Bringing ventilation up to current building regulations.
  • Making good the ceiling below if pipework needs to be accessed from underneath.

Getting an accurate quote

Because so much of a bathroom renovation happens behind the tiles, an accurate quote needs a site visit rather than a phone estimate. We'll check the existing plumbing, discuss layout options and give you an itemised price.
