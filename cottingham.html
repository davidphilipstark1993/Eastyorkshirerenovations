Cottingham homes

Renovating Cottingham’s villas, semis and rental homes.

Cottingham is often described as the largest village in England, and its houses reflect that size. Victorian villas line Hallgate and Northgate, solid inter-war semis fill much of the village, and newer family estates sit off Harland Way and towards the edges. Being next to the University of Hull, it also has plenty of rented houses.

Each of those needs something different. The older villas usually need careful replastering, updated wiring and a check for damp before anything new goes in. In the inter-war semis, opening the kitchen into the dining room is a common way to get a proper kitchen-diner. Rental houses tend to need a faster refresh between tenancies: see property refurbishment.

Planning in Cottingham

Planning and conservation in Cottingham.

Like Hessle, Cottingham has a Hull postcode (HU16) but is in the East Riding, so planning and building control go through East Riding of Yorkshire Council. Parts of the older village centre are in a conservation area, where changes to the outside of a house can need permission. We check this at the site visit.
