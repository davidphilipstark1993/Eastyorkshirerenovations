Cottingham property types

Renovating Cottingham's villas, semis and rental homes.

Cottingham is often described as the largest village in England, and its housing reflects that mix of scale: Victorian villas along Hallgate and Northgate, solid inter-war semis, and newer family estates off Harland Way and towards the outskirts of the village. Its proximity to the University of Hull also means a steady mix of family homes and rental properties, particularly around the centre.

Kitchen renovations are some of the most common work we carry out in Cottingham - the shaker-style kitchen we recently fitted locally, with marble-effect worktops and a new layout, is a good example of the kind of full refit homeowners here are asking for. We also see regular demand for bathroom upgrades and full refurbishments in the village's older villas, alongside quicker turnaround kitchen and bathroom refreshes for rental properties.

We cover Cottingham and the surrounding area for kitchen, bathroom, plastering and full renovation work.
