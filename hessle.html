Hessle property types

Renovating Hessle's terraces and family homes.

As a Hessle-based team, this is the area we know best. Hessle Square and the foreshore near the Humber Bridge are lined with Victorian and Edwardian terraces, many of which still have original room layouts that benefit from re-plastering, rewiring and modernised kitchens and bathrooms. Further from the town centre, around Hessle High Road and the newer estates towards Priory Park, you'll find larger 1970s-80s semis and detached homes with more straightforward renovation needs.

We see a lot of bathroom and wet room renovations in Hessle - the marble-effect tiling and walk-in shower conversions we've fitted locally are typical of what homeowners here are asking for, alongside kitchen refits and full refurbishments of older terraced housing near the town centre.

Being based locally means faster response times for quotes and site visits, and a good working knowledge of the older housing stock around Hessle Square and Hessle Foreshore.
