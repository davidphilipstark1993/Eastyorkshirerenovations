Our home town

What being based in Hessle means for your job.

Because we’re in the town, a site visit in Hessle is a few minutes’ drive rather than a planned trip. That makes it easy to come back and measure twice before a kitchen is ordered, look again at a damp patch after heavy rain, or keep a close eye on a job while it’s running.

One thing that surprises people: Hessle has a Hull postcode (HU13) but it is in the East Riding, not the city. Planning permission and building control go through East Riding of Yorkshire Council, not Hull City Council.

Hessle homes

Renovating Hessle’s older terraces and family homes.

Hessle is its own town on the north bank of the Humber, with the north end of the Humber Bridge on its doorstep. The older houses are around the town centre and The Square and down towards the foreshore: Victorian and Edwardian terraces and villas with solid brick walls. In these we expect to find tired or blown plaster, older wiring, and damp, especially in ground-floor rooms and rear additions.

Around the centre are inter-war and post-war semis and bungalows, and newer estates on the edges of town. The work there is more often a new kitchen, a bathroom or ensuite, or a garage turned into a room.

Damp surveys in Hessle.

Older solid-walled houses close to the estuary are prone to penetrating damp and condensation, and rising damp is often blamed when it isn’t the cause.
