Hessle property types

Renovating Hessle's terraces and family homes.

We're based in Hessle, on Station Road, so this is the area we know best. Hessle Square and the foreshore near the Humber Bridge are lined with Victorian and Edwardian terraces, many of which still have original room layouts that benefit from re-plastering, rewiring and a modernised kitchen and bathroom. Away from the centre, around Hessle High Road and the newer streets towards Priory Park, you'll find larger 1970s and 1980s semis and detached homes with more straightforward needs.

Hessle sits on the north bank of the Humber, and the older houses near the foreshore take wind-driven rain off the estuary. The damp we see in these terraces is often penetrating damp through weathered pointing and tired gutters, rising damp where paths and yards have been raised over the damp-proof course, and condensation in rooms where fans and vents have never been added. A survey sorts out which of those you actually have before anything is treated.

Bathrooms and wet rooms are the work we see most in Hessle. The marble-effect tiling and walk-in shower conversions we've fitted locally are typical of what homeowners here ask for, alongside kitchen refits and full refurbishments of older terraces near the town centre.

Being local means faster quotes and site visits, and a good working knowledge of the housing around Hessle Square and the foreshore. No two jobs are alike: a foreshore terrace may need replastering and rewiring as well as a new kitchen and bathroom, while a 1980s semi may only need one room updating, so we price each job after a site visit. A damp survey costs £119 including VAT, deducted from the cost of treatment if you go ahead, and we do the replastering and decorating ourselves.
