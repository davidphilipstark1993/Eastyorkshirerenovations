Garage conversions

Garage to bedroom conversions.

Turning a garage into a bedroom needs a bit more care than most conversions — sound insulation, a compliant escape route and heating all matter more in a room people sleep in.

Request a Free Quote View Our Recent Projects

What's different about this conversion

What matters most in a garage-to-bedroom conversion.

Fire safety & escape. A habitable bedroom needs an adequate means of escape, which usually means a window of a minimum size that can be opened for escape or rescue, particularly if the room doesn't open directly onto a hallway with its own escape route. We assess this specifically for bedroom conversions, since it's a stricter requirement than for an office or playroom.

Sound insulation. Because garages typically share a party wall with a neighbouring house (on semis and terraces) or sit close to the main living areas, upgrading sound insulation in the walls and party wall is worth prioritising for a bedroom, more so than for a lower-traffic room like a playroom.

Heating & comfort. We generally specify a higher, more consistent heating standard for a bedroom than for an office or gym, since comfort at night matters more than in a room only used during the day.

En-suite potential. Where an integral garage sits near existing bathroom plumbing, adding a small en-suite is often worth considering as part of the same project, particularly for a guest bedroom.

Frequently asked questions

Bedroom FAQs.

Does a garage-to-bedroom conversion need a special window for fire safety?

Often, yes. A habitable bedroom typically needs an adequate means of escape, which usually means a window that meets minimum size and opening requirements. We assess this specifically as part of the design.

Is soundproofing important for a garage bedroom conversion?

Yes, more so than for other room types, particularly where the garage shares a party wall with a neighbouring property.

Can you add an en-suite to a garage-to-bedroom conversion?

Where plumbing can be run from a nearby existing point, yes, this is a common addition for a guest bedroom conversion.
