Garage conversions

Garage to playroom conversions.

A converted garage makes a durable, easy-to-clean family playroom, separate from the main living areas so toys, mess and noise stay contained.

Request a Free Quote View Our Recent Projects

What's different about this conversion

What matters most in a garage-to-playroom conversion.

Durable, easy-clean finishes. Playrooms take more day-to-day wear than most rooms, so we typically specify hard-wearing, easy-to-clean flooring and washable wall finishes rather than the higher-spec materials used in a bedroom or office conversion.

Storage. Built-in shelving and storage cupboards are worth planning in from the start, both to keep the room tidy and to make the most of a typically compact garage footprint.

Noise containment. Sound insulation is still worth including, mainly to keep noise from the playroom contained rather than travelling through to the rest of the house.

Safety. Standard safety considerations apply, including socket covers, safety glazing where relevant, and rounded or protected edges on any built-in furniture.

Frequently asked questions

Playroom FAQs.

What flooring works best for a garage playroom?

Durable, easy-to-clean options such as vinyl or laminate are common choices, prioritising practicality over the higher-spec finishes used in a bedroom or office conversion.

Can you add built-in storage to a playroom conversion?

Yes, built-in shelving and storage cupboards are a common addition and help make the most of a typically compact garage footprint.

Will a playroom conversion need much insulation?

The same insulation standard as other garage conversions applies for comfort, with sound insulation particularly useful for containing noise from the rest of the house.
