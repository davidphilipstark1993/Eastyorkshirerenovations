Garages in Goole

Converting a garage in Goole.

Goole is part of the East Riding, so any planning questions go to East Riding of Yorkshire Council. Converting an integral garage within the existing footprint is usually an internal change of use that doesn’t need planning permission, but some newer estates have planning conditions that remove permitted development rights, so we check the position for your address before quoting. Building regulations still apply to the structural, insulation, damp-proofing and fire safety work.

The town sits on low-lying ground beside the River Ouse, so getting the garage floor and walls damp-proofed properly matters. Where the garage floor sits below the house’s damp-proof course, we assess it during the survey and price in the damp-proofing needed.

We travel to Goole from our base in Hessle along the M62, and we’ve already renovated a bathroom in nearby Howden.

We cover homes within about 30 miles of Hessle, which takes in all of Goole and Old Goole and the villages around it: Hook, Airmyn, Rawcliffe, Snaith, Swinefleet, Reedness, Howden, Barmby on the Marsh, Eastrington and Gilberdyke. These are all in the East Riding. We also work in Thorne, Crowle, Drax and Selby, where planning questions go to City of Doncaster Council, North Lincolnshire Council and North Yorkshire Council respectively.

Frequently asked questions

Garage conversion FAQs for Goole homeowners.

Do you cover Goole?

Yes. We’re based in Hessle and travel to Goole along the M62. Send us a few details and we’ll arrange a site visit.

Do I need planning permission to convert my garage in Goole?

Usually not for an integral garage, since it is typically an internal change of use. Some newer estates have conditions that remove permitted development rights, so we check the planning position for your address before quoting. Building regulations apply either way.

Will damp be a problem in my garage?

It can be, particularly where the garage floor sits below the house’s damp-proof course. We assess this during the survey and price in the necessary damp-proofing.

How much does a garage conversion cost in Goole?

It depends on the size of the garage, what it becomes, and how much insulation, damp-proofing, heating and electrical work is needed. We give a written, itemised quote after a site visit, and our cost guide explains each factor.
