Hull property types

Renovating Hull's terraces, villas and suburban homes.

Hull's housing stock is dominated by Victorian and Edwardian terraces, from the tightly packed streets around Newland Avenue and Princes Avenue to the grander bay-fronted villas of the Avenues conservation area. Further out, you'll find 1930s semis in areas like Kingswood and Bricknell, post-war estates, and newer developments around the Marina and Fruit Market.

Older Hull terraces often come with the same recurring issues: original lath-and-plaster walls and ceilings that need patching or replacing, dated bathrooms squeezed into small box rooms, and kitchens that haven't been touched since the 1990s. Solid brick construction close to the Humber estuary also means damp-proofing and ventilation are worth getting right, particularly in ground-floor rooms and rear extensions.

We regularly carry out kitchen and bathroom renovations, re-plastering and full refurbishments across Hull, working around the layout constraints typical of terraced housing - narrow hallways, back additions and box-room bathrooms included.
