Bathroom installs

Bathrooms fitted to last, not just to look good on day one.

A bathroom takes more punishment than any other room in the house, so getting the waterproofing right matters as much as the tiling. We tank shower areas properly, use the correct falls to drainage, and fit quality fixtures so you're not calling a plumber back six months later.

We fit full bathroom suites, ensuites and walk-in wet rooms across Hull, Hessle and East Yorkshire - from period terraces with awkward, boxed-in pipework to modern new-builds. That includes removing the old suite, first-fix plumbing and electrics, waterproofing, tiling floors and walls, and fitting the new bath, shower, basin, toilet and fixtures.

If you're working with a small or oddly shaped bathroom, we can advise on layouts that make the most of the space - corner showers, wall-hung vanity units and back-to-wall toilets all help a compact bathroom feel bigger.

Bathroom renovations in Hull

Bathrooms in Hull's terraces, villas and semis.

Much of Hull is Victorian and Edwardian terraces, from the streets around Newland Avenue and Princes Avenue to the bay-fronted villas of the Avenues, with 1930s semis around Bricknell and Anlaby Park and newer estates such as Kingswood. In the older terraces the bathroom is often squeezed into a box room or a back addition, with boxed-in pipework and lath-and-plaster walls behind the old tiles, so we plan the layout and the strip-out around what we find.

On a recent Hull bathroom renovation we stripped a dated bathroom back to the bare floorboards, then fully retiled and refitted it with a dark vanity unit and a glass shower screen. You can see more in our bathroom projects, including a walk-in shower in Hessle.

Showers and wet rooms

Shower and wet room installation in Hull.

Not every job is a full bathroom. We also replace an old bath with a walk-in shower, fit new shower enclosures and screens, and build level-access wet rooms. The part that matters is the part you can't see: the floor has to be strong and laid to a fall so water runs to the drain, the waste has to be able to take that fall, and the whole wet area is tanked before a tile goes on. In older Hull houses we often find rotten boards or old pipework under the bath, so we check before we price.

An electric shower needs its own circuit from the consumer unit, which we put in as part of the job (see our electrical work). A mixer shower depends on your boiler and water pressure, so we check both at the survey. You can see a finished example in our Hessle walk-in shower project.

How we work

How a bathroom install runs.

  1. Survey and quote

    We measure up, check the existing pipework, waste and ventilation, agree the layout and fittings with you, and give you a written quote.

  2. Strip-out

    We remove the old suite, tiles and any damaged plaster or boards, and protect the rest of the house while we work.

  3. Plumbing and electrics

    First fix: new pipework and waste runs, plus wiring for the extractor fan, lighting and any electric heating.

  4. Waterproofing

    Showers and wet areas are tanked with a waterproofing system before any tiles go on.

  5. Tiling

    Walls and floors are tiled and grouted, with sealant applied where it is needed.

  6. Fit-out

    The suite, furniture, taps, screen and towel rail go in, and everything is connected and tested.

  7. Snagging and handover

    We check every detail with you, put right anything that needs it and leave the room clean.

How much does a bathroom renovation cost in Hull?

A typical full bathroom renovation we carry out comes to around £6,000. Room size, suite quality, tiling extent, wet room or standard shower, and the condition of the existing plumbing and electrics all move the price, so we give an itemised written quote after seeing the room. Our cost guide explains each factor and the hidden costs to budget for.

Can you fit a bathroom I have already bought?

Yes. We fit suites and tiles you have chosen yourself as well as ones we help you pick. Tell us what you have when you ask for a quote so it is allowed for.

Do you do the plumbing, electrics and tiling yourselves?

You get one point of contact who manages the whole job, including first-fix plumbing and electrics, waterproofing, tiling and fitting the suite.

Can you replace a bath with a walk-in shower?

Yes. We check the floor and that the waste can take the fall a shower tray or wet-room floor needs, then tank the area before tiling. An electric shower also needs its own circuit, which we fit as part of the job.

Do you build wet rooms?

Yes. A wet room needs the floor laid to a fall towards the drain and the whole room waterproofed, not just the shower area, so we survey the floor structure first and include that in the quote.

Do you cover areas outside Hull?

Yes. We cover homes within about 30 miles of our Hessle base: Hessle, Beverley, Cottingham, Anlaby and the wider East Riding, Goole, and North Lincolnshire towns such as Barton-upon-Humber, Brigg and Scunthorpe. See the areas we cover.
