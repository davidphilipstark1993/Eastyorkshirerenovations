Bathroom installs

Bathrooms fitted to last, not just to look good on day one.

A bathroom takes more punishment than any other room in the house, so getting the waterproofing right matters as much as the tiling. We tank shower areas properly, use the correct falls to drainage, and fit quality fixtures so you're not calling a plumber back six months later.

We fit full bathroom suites, ensuites and walk-in wet rooms across Hull, Hessle and East Yorkshire - from period terraces with awkward, boxed-in pipework to modern new-builds. That includes removing the old suite, first-fix plumbing and electrics, waterproofing, tiling floors and walls, and fitting the new bath, shower, basin, toilet and fixtures.

If you're working with a small or oddly shaped bathroom, we can advise on layouts that make the most of the space - corner showers, wall-hung vanity units and back-to-wall toilets all help a compact bathroom feel bigger.

What's included

A full bathroom install typically covers:

  • Removal and disposal of the old suite
  • First-fix plumbing and electrics, including extractor fans
  • Tanking and waterproofing to shower and wet areas
  • Wall and floor tiling
  • Fitting the new bath, shower, basin, toilet and fixtures
  • Silicone sealing and final snagging

How we work

How a bathroom install runs.

  1. Survey and quote

    We measure up, check the existing pipework, waste and ventilation, agree the layout and fittings with you, and give you a written quote.

  2. Strip-out

    We remove the old suite, tiles and any damaged plaster or boards, and protect the rest of the house while we work.

  3. Plumbing and electrics

    First fix: new pipework and waste runs, plus wiring for the extractor fan, lighting and any electric heating.

  4. Waterproofing

    Showers and wet areas are tanked with a waterproofing system before any tiles go on.

  5. Tiling

    Walls and floors are tiled and grouted, with sealant applied where it is needed.

  6. Fit-out

    The suite, furniture, taps, screen and towel rail go in, and everything is connected and tested.

  7. Snagging and handover

    We check every detail with you, put right anything that needs it and leave the room clean.
