Anlaby property types

Renovating Anlaby's semis, bungalows and family homes.

Anlaby is largely made up of inter-war and post-war semis and bungalows, with pockets of newer housing towards Anlaby Retail Park and Springfield Way. As a commuter suburb close to Hull, it's popular with families and downsizers alike, and a lot of the housing stock still has original 1970s-80s kitchens and bathrooms in need of modernising.

Typical jobs in Anlaby include full kitchen and bathroom refits, re-plastering after removing dated coving or textured ceilings, and general decorating to bring older interiors up to date. Bungalows in the area often need loft insulation or conversion work considered alongside any renovation, given the more limited floor space compared to a two-storey semi.

We cover Anlaby and the surrounding western Hull suburbs for kitchen, bathroom, plastering and decorating work.
