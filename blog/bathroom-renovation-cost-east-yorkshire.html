How Much Does a Bathroom Renovation Cost in East Yorkshire?

What drives bathroom renovation pricing in Hull and East Yorkshire, and the hidden costs worth planning for.

Get a Quote More guides

Bathroom renovation costs vary more than most people expect, mainly because of what's happening behind the tiles rather than what you can see. This guide covers the main cost drivers so you can budget realistically before getting quotes.

What affects the cost of a bathroom renovation

  • Room size: a small ensuite costs less to renovate than a full family bathroom.
  • Suite quality: budget bath, basin and toilet suites cost less than premium fixtures and designer fittings.
  • Wet room vs standard shower: a level-access wet room conversion involves more structural and waterproofing work than fitting a shower over a bath.
  • Tiling extent: full-height tiling on every wall costs more than half-height tiling with painted walls above.
  • Waterproofing and tanking: proper tanking behind tiled shower areas is essential and adds to the cost, but it's not somewhere to cut corners.
  • Moving plumbing: relocating the toilet, bath or basin adds plumbing work compared with replacing fittings in the same positions.
  • Ventilation: extractor fan installation or upgrades, particularly in bathrooms without an external window.

Hidden costs to budget for

  • Rotten or damaged subfloor discovered once old flooring is removed.
  • Old plumbing needing full replacement rather than reuse.
  • Bringing ventilation up to current building regulations.
  • Making good the ceiling below if pipework needs to be accessed from underneath.
