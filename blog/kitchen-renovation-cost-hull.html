How Much Does a Kitchen Renovation Cost in Hull?

A guide to what affects kitchen renovation pricing in Hull and East Yorkshire, and the questions worth asking before you budget.

Get a Quote More guides

Online cost calculators and national "average kitchen cost" articles rarely reflect what you'll actually pay locally, because so much depends on the size of your kitchen, the scope of work and the finish you choose. This guide walks through the main factors so you know what questions to ask when you get quotes.

What affects the cost of a kitchen renovation

  • Size and layout: a small galley kitchen costs less to fit than a large open-plan kitchen-diner, and moving the layout around (relocating a sink or hob) adds plumbing and electrical work.
  • Structural work: knocking through to a dining room or hallway adds building work and potentially a structural beam, on top of the kitchen fit itself.
  • Cabinet material: MDF and laminate cabinets cost less than solid wood or painted shaker-style units.
  • Worktop material: laminate is the most affordable option; quartz, granite and marble-effect worktops cost more but are more durable.
  • Appliances: integrated, built-in appliances cost more than freestanding ones, and the number of appliances (oven, hob, extractor, dishwasher, fridge-freezer) adds up.
  • Tiling extent: a splashback behind the hob costs less than full wall tiling.
  • Condition of existing plumbing and electrics: older properties sometimes need first-fix work redone before a new kitchen can go in.

Hidden costs to budget for

  • Re-plastering walls and ceilings once old units and tiling are removed.
  • Consumer unit or electrical upgrades if the existing wiring doesn't meet current regulations.
  • Flooring repairs or replacement once old units are stripped out.
  • Skip hire and waste disposal.
  • Temporary kitchen facilities if you're staying in the property during the work.
