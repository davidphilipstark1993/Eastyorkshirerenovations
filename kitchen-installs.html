Kitchen installs

Kitchens built around the way you live.

A kitchen renovation touches almost every trade in the house: joinery, plumbing, electrics, plastering and tiling all have to come together in the right order. We manage that sequence for you, so cabinetry goes in plumb and level, worktops are templated and fitted accurately, and appliances, sockets and lighting end up exactly where you need them.

Whether you're replacing a dated 1990s kitchen in a Hull terrace, opening up a galley kitchen into a family dining space, or fitting a brand-new kitchen in a self-build or extension, we handle the full process - from first-fix plumbing and electrics through to the final silicone line around the worktop.

We work with a range of budgets and cabinet suppliers, and we're happy to fit a kitchen you've already ordered or help you choose one. Either way, you get a single point of contact for the whole project rather than juggling separate joiners, plumbers, electricians and tilers yourself.

What's included

A full kitchen install typically covers:

  • Removal and disposal of the old kitchen
  • First-fix plumbing for sinks, dishwashers and washing machines
  • First-fix electrics for sockets, lighting and appliance circuits
  • Cabinet installation, levelling and worktop fitting
  • Splashback tiling and finishing trims
  • Appliance installation and connection
