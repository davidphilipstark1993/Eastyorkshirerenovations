## Subject: Trade/dealer enquiry — East Yorkshire Renovation, home water treatment

Dear [Supplier contact name / Trade team],

My name is [Name], and I run East Yorkshire Renovation, an established home renovation business
based in Hessle, East Yorkshire (eastyorkshirerenovation.com). We're researching the addition of
a home water treatment service — water softening, whole-home filtration and drinking-water
systems — alongside our existing kitchen, bathroom and full-house renovation work, and I'd like
to understand whether [Supplier] would be able to support us as a trade/dealer partner.

Could you please let me know:

**Trade terms**
1. Do you supply equipment to independent home-improvement/renovation businesses on a trade or
   dealer account basis?
2. What are the requirements to open a trade account (e.g. minimum order value/quantity,
   business registration, credit terms)?
3. Is trade pricing available on request, and if so, could you share an indicative price list
   for your domestic softener and filtration range?

**Installer/dealer requirements**
4. Do you require installers to complete any training or certification before fitting your
   equipment, and if so, what does that involve (cost, duration, format)?
5. Do you require the installing business/individual to hold a specific qualification or
   registration (e.g. WaterSafe) as a condition of your warranty, or is that left to us to
   arrange independently?
6. Is there a defined installation and commissioning process/checklist you require dealers to
   follow?

**Territory and exclusivity**
7. Would appointing us as a dealer in the Hull/East Yorkshire area involve any territorial
   exclusivity — for us, for you, or for other dealers already operating nearby?
8. Are we able to sell other manufacturers' equipment alongside yours, or does your dealer
   agreement require exclusivity to your brand?

**Support**
9. Do you offer any lead-generation or marketing support to dealers (e.g. inclusion in an online
   dealer/postcode finder, co-op advertising, marketing materials)?
10. Is finance (e.g. 0% consumer finance) available to dealers to offer their customers, and if
    so, how does that work commercially for us?

**Consumables and parts**
11. What are your trade prices for salt, filter cartridges and (where relevant) RO membranes?
12. What is typical parts/spares availability and lead time for servicing/repairs?

**Warranty and servicing**
13. What warranty terms apply to equipment sold through dealers (parts, labour, duration), and
    are there any conditions that could void the warranty (e.g. installation by a non-approved
    installer, use of non-OEM salt/cartridges)?
14. Do you require dealers to offer a specific servicing schedule to maintain the warranty?

**Compliance**
15. Which of your domestic softener/filtration products currently hold WRAS/WaterRegsUK
    approval or listing, and can you point us to the relevant documentation per model?
16. Do you have technical documentation covering compliant installation under the Water Supply
    (Water Fittings) Regulations 1999 (backflow protection, servicing valves) for your products?

We're at an early research stage and haven't committed to a supplier yet — we're comparing a
small number of manufacturers before deciding how to proceed, and we'd value the chance to speak
with someone on your trade/dealer team.

Thank you for your time — I look forward to hearing from you.

Kind regards,
[Name]
East Yorkshire Renovation
[Phone] / [Email]
eastyorkshirerenovation.com
