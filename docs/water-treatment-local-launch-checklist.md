# Water Treatment — Local Launch Checklist (Manual Actions)

These are things for a person to do, not code changes. Nothing here is automated - do not
connect any tool to Google Business Profile without deciding to do so explicitly.

## Google Business Profile
- [ ] Add "Water Softener Installation Service" / "Water Treatment" as a service or category on
      the existing East Yorkshire Renovations Google Business Profile, if the platform allows
      adding a service without changing the primary business category.
- [ ] Add a Google Business Profile **post** linking to `/water-treatment.html`, using the same
      honest tone as the website (no scare tactics, no invented claims).
- [ ] Confirm the profile's listed service areas genuinely match what's on the website: Hull,
      Beverley, Hessle, Cottingham, Anlaby, Willerby, and the wider East Riding.
- [ ] If/when genuine photography exists (see below), add real photos to the profile - not stock
      images, not the CSS placeholders used on the website.

## Reviews
- [ ] Collect genuine reviews over time, starting with the first real water-treatment customers
      (see `docs/water-treatment-first-lead-test.md`). Ask via Google Business Profile's own
      review link so they're independently verifiable, rather than typing a quote onto the
      website yourself.
- [ ] Do not add reviews to the website until they are real and verifiable. The homepage's
      previous placeholder reviews were removed this session for exactly this reason - see
      `docs/water-treatment-conversion-audit.md` and the final report for that task.

## Local content
- [ ] Once there's real experience to draw on (a handful of completed jobs), consider the local
      landing pages already scoped in the prior business-model research
      (`/water-treatment-hull.html` etc.) - only once there's genuine local detail to add, not as
      thin, near-duplicate pages.
- [ ] The content roadmap from the prior research session
      (`docs/water-treatment-business-model.md`, Part 17) lists specific topics worth writing -
      revisit that list once there's bandwidth, rather than writing speculative content now.

## Photography
- [ ] Replace the "Before & After" section's CSS placeholders on `/water-treatment.html` with
      real photography once available - the exact file-naming convention is documented in that
      section's HTML comments and in `assets/img/water-treatment/README.md`.

## Do not do automatically
- Do not connect any Google Business Profile API integration for this test.
- Do not buy or request reviews, or offer incentives for them - just ask genuine customers,
  honestly, after real work is completed.
