# Commercial strategy: which services to push (2026-10-17)

Evidence base: the quotes and invoices in the owner's business folder
(customer names left out here), the live site, and the site's own pricing.
**Not available: job costs, labour hours, or margins.** The ranking below is
therefore a reasoned view, not a profit calculation. Fill the gaps in the last
section and re-rank.

## What the paperwork shows
- Work to date is mostly B2B: letting agents and landlords (works orders and
  maintenance quotes of roughly £800 to £4,000, a refurb at £14,400 inc VAT), plus
  some private bathrooms (£1,350 final payment on one; a £5,650 bathroom).
- Those jobs are bundles of small trades (decorating, doors, flooring,
  mould treatment, minor damp). Damp surveys and specialist damp treatment
  do not appear as priced jobs yet.
- Letting-agent work arrives without ad spend but is price-competitive,
  slow to pay, and often quoted at low or nil margin on small items.

## Priority list
| Rank | Service | Why | Channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Damp surveys (£119 fixed) leading to damp treatment and making good | Fixed low-friction entry price, strong local search intent ("damp survey Hull"), the owner does the survey himself so no subcontract cost, and a survey creates the follow-on remedial and replastering job in the same visit | Meta ads to the book-a-survey page, Google Business Profile, damp guides |
| 2 | Landlord / letting-agent damp & mould reports | Repeat, recurring volume from the agents already working with the business; legal pressure on landlords on damp and mould is rising | Direct outreach to existing agents, not ads |
| 3 | Pre-purchase damp surveys | Time-pressured buyers who book fast; report ready in 3 days | Search, estate-agent and solicitor referrals |
| 4 | Bathrooms (and kitchens) | Highest ticket among private-homeowner jobs (£5k+) and the strongest photo evidence in the folder; but labour-heavy, longer sales cycle and trade subcontracting | Organic search, Google reviews, project pages |
| 5 | Water treatment | Unproven demand, no supplier or costs confirmed, equipment margin unknown. Keep as a demand test | Low-spend test only |
| 6 | Garden rooms, orangeries, garage conversions, conservatory work, outdoor kitchens | The site covers these with guides, but there is no evidence in the paperwork of completed jobs, and they are high-risk, high-capital jobs. Do not spend ad money here yet | Organic only |

## What to do
- Spend ad money only on rank 1 until you have 10 booked surveys of data.
- Keep the other service pages for search and credibility; do not expand them.
- Track every lead by service line (see growth-reporting-and-plan.md).

days on site, % of quotes accepted, and how often payment is late.
