# Water Treatment Competitor Research

Research date: September 2026. Compiled from live web research (search + direct fetches of
official company/manufacturer pages). All figures are labelled:

- **PUBLISHED / VERIFIED** — found stated directly on an official company/manufacturer page.
- **ESTIMATED / MARKET RANGE** — third-party aggregator/comparison-site figures, not confirmed
  on an official page. Treat as indicative only.
- **NOT FOUND** — could not be confirmed from available sources. Do not assume a figure.

Nothing in this document should be copied into marketing claims without re-verification closer
to launch — prices and offers change.

### 5. UK Water Softeners (market context, not a single confirmed local entity)
- Search results returned generic UK-wide cost-guide content (MyJobQuote, MyBuilder,
  Checkatrade, etc.) rather than a single "UK Water Softeners" trading company with its own
  distinct offer in East Yorkshire specifically. ESTIMATED / MARKET RANGE figures pulled from
  these aggregator sites (not official company pages, treat as broad market context only):
  - Typical UK **installed** cost: **£800–£2,500**.
  - Labour alone: **£175–£650** (or £200–£450 depending on source — ranges vary by aggregator).
  - Salt-based ion-exchange softener unit only: **£400–£1,000**.
  - Salt-free systems: £500–£1,500. Dual-tank: £1,000–£2,000+. Magnetic/electronic descalers:
    £100–£400 (note: descalers are a different technology to ion-exchange softening and make
    different, more contested claims — we should not sell these as equivalent to softening).
  - Ongoing salt costs: **£100–£180/year** (ESTIMATED / MARKET RANGE).
- **Source (aggregators, not manufacturer-verified):** [MyJobQuote](https://www.myjobquote.co.uk/costs/water-softener-installation), [MyBuilder](https://www.mybuilder.com/plumbing/price-guides/water-softeners-cost), [Checkatrade](https://www.checkatrade.com/blog/cost-guides/water-softener-cost/)

## National brands

### 6. Kinetico UK (kinetico.co.uk)
- **Main products:** Non-electric twin-tank water softeners (Premier Compact, Premier Plus),
  drinking-water systems, whole-home filtration.
- **Retail prices:** **NOT FOUND published** on the official product page — Kinetico is a
  quote-only, dealer-network model. Third-party dealer sites show real transacted examples:
  Aquastream (authorised dealer) lists a range up to **£1,182**; multiple dealers advertise
  "£300 off RRP" on Premier Plus/Premier Compact (ESTIMATED / MARKET RANGE, dealer-published,
  not Kinetico's own RRP).
- **Finance:** **"Spread the cost with 0% finance"** (PUBLISHED, official product page).
- **Warranty:** **10-year parts warranty** (PUBLISHED, official product page).
- **Installation:** Official page states installation by trained Kinetico professionals,
  typically ~4 hours, preceded by a pre-installation assessment visit (PUBLISHED).
- **Postcode/dealer finder:** Yes — enter postcode to find local dealer (PUBLISHED,
  [kinetico.co.uk/where-to-buy](https://www.kinetico.co.uk/where-to-buy)). Kinetico also
  publishes a **"Hard Water by Postcode"** content page
  ([kinetico.co.uk/hard-water-by-postcode](https://www.kinetico.co.uk/hard-water-by-postcode)).
- **Business model:** Operates via an authorised dealer network (regional exclusivity implied
  by "find your local dealer"), not direct national sales — relevant to our own supplier
  strategy (see supplier research doc).
- **Strengths:** Strong brand recognition, non-electric twin-tank technology is a genuine
  technical differentiator (continuous soft water, no downtime during regeneration), 10-year
  warranty, 0% finance already normalised in this market.
- **Weaknesses:** No public pricing anywhere — pure lead-gen/dealer-quote model, which may
  frustrate price-comparing customers.
- **Source:** [kinetico.co.uk/products/water-softeners/premier](https://www.kinetico.co.uk/products/water-softeners/premier), [kinetico.co.uk/where-to-buy](https://www.kinetico.co.uk/where-to-buy)

### 7. Harvey / Culligan Harvey (harveywatersofteners.co.uk) and TwinTec
- **Main products:** Harvey-branded softeners sold direct; **TwinTec** is Harvey's
  separately-branded dealer-network product line (twintec.com) — Harvey effectively runs two
  go-to-market channels under one manufacturer.
- **Retail prices (direct):** **NOT FOUND published** on Harvey's own FAQ page — official page
  explicitly declines to quote a number and instead ties price to household size, plumbing,
  water usage and requires an in-home "professional demonstrator" assessment (PUBLISHED
  wording/approach, not a price).
- **Retail prices (TwinTec, via independent dealers):** TwinTec S4 unit commonly listed at
  **£1,149–£1,174** by independent online dealers, sometimes bundled with "2-year home-attend
  labour warranty + 10 years parts" (ESTIMATED / MARKET RANGE — dealer-published, not Harvey's
  own RRP).
- **Installed cost (direct Harvey channel):** ESTIMATED / MARKET RANGE from aggregators:
  **£1,800–£2,600** typical installed quote through Harvey's own sales channel — markedly
  higher than the TwinTec independent-dealer route (**£1,000–£1,400** installed, same
  aggregator sources). This direct-vs-dealer price gap is a genuinely useful structural insight.
- **Finance:** Credit broker via **Mitsubishi HC Capital** (subject to approval) (PUBLISHED,
  official FAQ page).
- **Money-back guarantee:** **90-day money-back guarantee** on standard equipment (PUBLISHED,
  official FAQ page) — this is a real, named, evidenced example of a "no scare tactics, low
  risk" guarantee mechanism we could reference as a model (not copy) when designing our own
  trust proposition.
- **Warranty:** TwinTec dealer listings commonly cite 2-year labour + 10-year parts (ESTIMATED —
  dealer-published, verify against Harvey's own terms before quoting).
- **Strengths:** Britain's best-known softener brand; genuine money-back guarantee; strong
  demonstrator-led sales process; 0%-style finance via a real credit broker.
- **Weaknesses:** No public pricing on the flagship brand at all; direct-channel pricing appears
  to carry a significant premium over the same underlying product sold via independent dealers.
- **Source:** [harveywatersofteners.co.uk/faq/how-much-does-a-harvey-water-softener-cost/](https://www.harveywatersofteners.co.uk/faq/how-much-does-a-harvey-water-softener-cost/), [twintec.com](https://www.twintec.com/), aggregator context: [Manchester Herald](https://manchesterherald.co.uk/harvey-softener-price/) (ESTIMATED figures only)

## Key findings vs. our current provisional pricing

Our current placeholder ESSENTIAL package price is **£1,995**. The two most directly comparable
local competitors with published fully-installed entry prices are:

- Greens Water Systems (Hull showroom): **£957 fully installed**
- Aquarius Water Softeners (regional): **£895 fully fitted**

Both are less than half our current placeholder. This is the single most important finding of
the competitor research and is addressed directly in `docs/water-treatment-unit-economics.md`
and the Part 8 repricing decision.

## Common customer-facing patterns worth adopting (not copying wording/branding)
1. A genuine free, no-obligation home survey before any commitment — near-universal.
2. Warranty length (10 years parts) is a normalised market expectation, not a differentiator.
3. Finance (0% or broker-based) appears at the national-brand tier but not confirmed at the
   local-independent tier — a possible opportunity for us to differentiate locally.
4. None of the researched competitors publish a genuine postcode-level *hardness* lookup with
   real-time data — Kinetico publishes hardness-by-postcode as informational content, and
   several third-party sites (WaterHard.uk, TapWater.uk, Filter Flair) offer postcode checkers,
   but the only checker tied to an actual water company account (rather than a generic modelled
   dataset) is Yorkshire Water's own tool — see the compliance/data-source note in
   `docs/water-treatment-compliance.md` and Part 15 below.
5. Multiple competitors explicitly avoid overtly "salesy" language and lean on "no obligation,"
   "professional demonstrator/survey," and long warranties as trust mechanisms rather than fear.
