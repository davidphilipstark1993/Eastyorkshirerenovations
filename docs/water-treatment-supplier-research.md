# Water Treatment Supplier Research

Research date: September 2026. This document researches potential UK equipment
suppliers/manufacturers/distributors — it does **not** recommend a purchase decision. No
supplier has been contacted. Where trade pricing is not publicly disclosed, this document says
so explicitly rather than estimating a number.

Labelling convention as in the competitor research document: **PUBLISHED / VERIFIED**,
**ESTIMATED / MARKET RANGE**, **NOT FOUND**.

## Cross-cutting compliance/operational questions (apply to every supplier above)

All of the following require confirmation directly from each supplier and/or the relevant
regulator before any commercial commitment — none should be assumed:

- Dealer/installer training requirements and whether training is supplier-provided or must be
  sourced independently.
- Minimum order quantities/values.
- Whether trade pricing requires a credit account, minimum annual spend, or upfront payment.
- Territory exclusivity — whether appointing us as a local dealer would restrict the supplier
  from also supplying our direct competitors (Hull Water Softeners, Greens, Aquarius) in the
  same postcodes, or vice versa restrict us to their brand only.
- Whether we could legally/commercially sell more than one brand simultaneously (a genuinely
  "independent advisor" positioning likely requires this — see business model document).
- Finance scheme availability at dealer level (vs. only on the manufacturer's own direct
  channel, as currently confirmed for Kinetico and Harvey).
- Lead-generation or marketing support (co-branded advertising funds, lead-sharing, listing on
  the manufacturer's own dealer-finder tool) — this is common in this industry (Kinetico,
  EcoWater and TwinTec all operate consumer-facing dealer finders) and could materially reduce
  our own customer-acquisition cost if available.
- Consumables economics: salt cost per bag/tonne at trade rate, filter cartridge cost, RO
  membrane cost, minimum reorder quantities.
- Water Regulations (WRAS) status of the specific product models under consideration — WRAS
  approval is at the *product* level, not the *company* level, so this must be checked per
  model, not assumed brand-wide. See `docs/water-treatment-compliance.md`.
- Whether installation/commissioning must be carried out by a specific class of qualified
  person per the supplier's own warranty terms (several manufacturers void warranty if
  installed by an unapproved installer — this must be checked per brand before we ever promise
  a warranty to a customer).

## Preliminary shortlist for supplier enquiry (subject to Part 24 template, not yet sent)

Based on findings above, the most promising initial enquiry targets — prioritised by (a)
confirmed openness to independent/dealer businesses and (b) relevance to our target price
band — are:

1. **Monarch Water** — explicitly trade-only distribution model; long-established UK
   manufacturer; likely most straightforward "yes we sell to businesses like you" conversation.
2. **TwinTec (Harvey)** — an established, named dealer network already exists in this exact
   category; gives access to the UK's best-known softener brand without needing Harvey's own
   direct-sales infrastructure.
3. **Aqua Cure** — explicit trade account programme, broad product range (softening +
   filtration + RO + consumables) covering our full package structure from one supplier, strong
   independent review trust signal (Trustpilot 4.8).
4. **EcoWater UK** — dealer network with an in-built assessment-led sales process already
   matching our intended positioning.
5. **Kinetico UK** — strongest brand recognition nationally; worth an exploratory enquiry even
   though entry requirements are unconfirmed, given the technology and 10-year warranty are
   genuinely differentiated.

This is a shortlist for **enquiry**, not a purchasing decision. See
