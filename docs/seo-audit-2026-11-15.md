## How the site is built and deployed

- Static HTML on Vercel. There is no framework or CMS and no build step on Vercel. The files in the repo are what gets served.
- About 100 pages:
  - Older pages are hand-edited `.html` files at the root: services and town pages.
  - Newer sections are `folder/index.html` pages: orangeries, garage conversions, garden rooms, conservatory transformations, outdoor kitchens, damp proofing, guides and projects.
- Node scripts in `scripts/` generate parts of the site:
  - Damp pages, guides, projects, About, privacy and 404 are generated.
  - The shared head, header, footer and business JSON-LD (`scripts/lib/site.mjs`) are stamped into every page between `<!-- site:NAME -->` markers.
  - Owner facts live in `scripts/data/business.mjs`.
  - `node scripts/build-all.mjs` rebuilds everything, including `sitemap.xml` and the guide redirects in `vercel.json`.
- Redirects live in `vercel.json` with `permanent: true`, so Vercel sends a 308. Google treats a 308 exactly like a 301.
- Forms post to the serverless functions in `api/`.
- Git: `main` is at PR #10 (merged 2 Nov 2026). Work goes on a new branch and through a PR, as before.

## Findings: real issues

1. **Apex domain uses a temporary redirect.** `eastyorkshirerenovation.com` → `www.` returns **307**; it should be 308 or 301. This is a Vercel dashboard setting (Project → Settings → Domains → edit the apex → "Redirect to www", choose 308). It can't be fixed in the repo. **Owner action.**
2. **Homepage and hub pages barely link to town pages.** The homepage, services.html, /orangeries/, /garage-conversions/, /garden-rooms/, /conservatory-transformations/ and /outdoor-kitchens/ each link to only 1 of the 10 town pages, through the footer's "East Riding" link. The .html service pages link to 5 of 10 (Willerby, Swanland, Brough, Kirk Ella and North Ferriby are missing). Town pages never link to joinery or electrical, and most don't link to decorating. This is the biggest structural gap.
3. **Guides don't link to local service pages.** Only the damp-survey-cost guide links to `/damp-proofing/hull/` etc. The other 9 damp guides, the conservatory and garage guides and the orangery-design guide (which links to Beverley only) have no contextual local links. Their only local mentions come from the shared header and footer.
4. **Orangery local pages don't cross-link.** `/orangeries/hull/` links to no sibling page, and `/orangeries/east-yorkshire/` doesn't link to Hull.
5. **Town pages are thin:** 380–460 words of templated copy, and Hull, Beverley, Cottingham, Hessle and Anlaby have no FAQ.
6. **Garage conversions: no Goole page.** Goole is in your `areaServed` list. `/garage-conversions/hull/` (523 words) has no FAQ or FAQ schema, and its title "Transform Your Garage" wastes characters.
7. **Joinery:** a single page (583 words) with no location context beyond "Hull & East Yorkshire", and only 3 of 12 town pages link to it.
8. **No bathroom price figures anywhere on the site.** The page deliberately says "we don't give a single figure". That's honest, but searchers want a ballpark. A price range can only be added if you supply one.
9. **Page speed:**
   - Content images are 1600px JPEG/WebP at 400–760KB, with no `srcset`. On a phone this is the largest cost.
   - The homepage hero (`kitchen-finished.jpg`) is already `fetchpriority="high"`, and below-the-fold images are lazy-loaded.
   - Render-blocking resources are only `styles.css` and the Google Fonts CSS; scripts are `defer`.
   - The October report found that *recompressing* saved under 4%. *Resizing* to 800/1200px variants with `srcset` would cut mobile weight much more.
10. **Homepage title and description** cover the right words but give no reason to click.
