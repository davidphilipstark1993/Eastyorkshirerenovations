Our coverage

The parts of Lincolnshire we cover, and the parts we don’t.

“Lincolnshire” covers three council areas. We work in two of them, within about 30 miles of our base in Hessle.

North Lincolnshire: covered

Barton-upon-Humber, Brigg, Scunthorpe, Winterton, Epworth and Crowle. See renovations in North Lincolnshire for the detail.

North East Lincolnshire: covered

Grimsby, Cleethorpes and Immingham. This is the far edge of our area, so we confirm each job by postcode before booking a visit.

The rest of Lincolnshire: not covered

We don’t normally take on work further south, such as Lincoln, Louth, Skegness, Boston or Grantham. It’s too far from Hessle for us to run a job properly. If you’re near the northern edge of the county, around Caistor, Market Rasen or Gainsborough, send your postcode and we’ll give you an honest answer.

Grimsby, Cleethorpes and Immingham

Renovating homes in North East Lincolnshire.

Grimsby has a lot of Victorian terraced housing around the East and West Marsh, built when the docks and fishing industry were at their height, and a large stock of inter-war and post-war homes across the town and out towards Cleethorpes. Cleethorpes has its own Victorian and Edwardian seaside streets, and Immingham is mostly twentieth-century housing that grew up around the port.

Sitting on the Humber estuary near the open coast, older houses in this area take a lot of wind-driven rain, so penetrating damp and condensation are the problems we expect to find most. Our damp surveys in Grimsby cover the whole area at £119. Planning and building control here are handled by North East Lincolnshire Council.

Frequently asked questions

Lincolnshire coverage: FAQs.

Do you cover Lincoln?

No. Lincoln is too far from our base in Hessle for us to run renovation work there properly. We cover North Lincolnshire and the Grimsby, Cleethorpes and Immingham area.

Do you cover Grimsby and Cleethorpes?

Yes, though they’re at the edge of our area, so we confirm each job by postcode first. Damp surveys there are £119, the same as everywhere else we cover.

What’s the difference between North Lincolnshire and Lincolnshire?

North Lincolnshire (Scunthorpe, Brigg, Barton) and North East Lincolnshire (Grimsby, Cleethorpes, Immingham) are separate councils from Lincolnshire County Council, which covers the rest of the county, including Lincoln. We work in the first two.

Do you have premises in Lincolnshire?

No. Our only base is Station Road in Hessle, and we travel over the Humber Bridge for every visit.
